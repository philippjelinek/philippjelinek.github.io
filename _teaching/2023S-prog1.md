Teaching Assistant (TA) responsible for supporting the exercise part of the course in cooperation with fellow tutors. The exercise part consists of a mandatory introductory exercise cycle followed by 4 graded exercises.

Duties included:
- Supervising the entry test 
- Assisting students during the elaboration of the lab assignments (Ausarbeitung der Laboraufgaben)
- Conducting the submission interviews in the lab


Subject of the course
======
Programming in C, programming step by step, structured programming, working with development systems, variables/constants, expressions in C, data-types in C, standard input/output, selections, iterations, functions, arrays, strings, structures, pointers and addresses, the preprocessor, input/output with files.
